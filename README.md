## QI TV Online: A Simple Guide to Watching

A film for tonight, a series to come back to, or a live channel to keep you company. QI TV Online brings the current Qi TV library into one browser page, with Arabic navigation and a quiet, readable layout.

[Open QI TV](https://wolfin.app/tv)

## 1. Open the library

Find QI TV Online on the Services page and choose **Open**. You can also go straight to [wolfin.app/tv](https://wolfin.app/tv). The page works on a computer or phone; you do not need to install another application to browse it.

![QI TV homepage with Discover, Movies, Series and Channels navigation](https://wolfin.app/media/qi-tv/qitv-home.jpg)

*The main screen. Use the navigation at the top, or choose اكتشف عالمك to reach the library.*

## 2. Find something you want to watch

Choose الأفلام for films, المسلسلات for series, or القنوات for live TV. The search field looks through the current library. Type part of a title, then narrow the results with the genre buttons. The sorting menu can arrange results by recent additions, rating, year or name.

Searching does not start a video. Choose a result to see its details first.

![Searching for Wild in the QI TV library with three matching results and genre filters](https://wolfin.app/media/qi-tv/qitv-search.jpg)

*A real search for “Wild”, with matching titles, genre filters and the sorting menu.*

## 3. Read the details and choose an episode

Open a poster to see the information supplied for that title. Depending on the source, this can include the year, rating, story, cast and running time. Some fields may be missing.

For a film, choose شغّل الفيلم. For a series, choose شغّل أول حلقة to begin with the first episode, or scroll down to اختار الموسم and select a season and episode. For a channel, choose شغّل البث المباشر.

![Wild Vietnam detail view with its poster, title, year and first-episode button](https://wolfin.app/media/qi-tv/qitv-details.jpg)

*The detail view lets you inspect a title before starting it.*

![Season selector and an episode in the Wild Vietnam detail view](https://wolfin.app/media/qi-tv/qitv-episodes.jpg)

*Scroll within a series detail view to find the season selector and published episodes.*

## 4. Make the player comfortable

The player includes play and pause, ten-second jumps, volume and full screen. When the stream provides them, the controls below the video offer picture quality, audio language and subtitles. A choice only appears when it is available in that stream.

For live TV, the timeline may be marked مباشر and seeking may be unavailable. On a phone, full-screen controls depend on the browser.

![QI TV player with playback controls, quality, audio and subtitle selections](https://wolfin.app/media/qi-tv/qitv-player.jpg)

*The playback controls and the available quality, audio and subtitle choices.*

## 5. Keep a short list

Choose the heart on a card, or أضف لقائمتي in its detail view. Open قائمتي in the library to see your saved choices. This list is stored in that browser on that device; clearing browser storage removes it.

## If a title does not start

Allow the first video segments to load. Use a current Safari or Chrome browser, and open the page directly in the browser if an in-app window has trouble playing protected video. Content availability, picture quality and playback protection depend on the source and browser support.

If a temporary maintenance message appears, try again later or refresh the library. A missing stream, subtitle track or source licence is not something the quality selector can restore.
